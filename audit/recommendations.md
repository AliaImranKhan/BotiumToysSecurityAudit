Recommendations and Possible Improvements:

Based on the security audit, Botium Toys has several areas that need improvement. The main recommendations are focused on protecting sensitive data, controlling access, maintaining business operations, and improving compliance.

1. Implement Least Privilege

Limit employee access to systems and data based on their job responsibilities. Currently, employees have access to sensitive information such as PII/SPII and cardholder data.

Why: This reduces the risk of unauthorized access to sensitive information.

2. Implement Encryption

Encrypt sensitive customer and payment information, especially credit-card data stored internally.

Why: Encryption helps protect confidential information if unauthorized access occurs.

3. Create Backups

Establish regular backups for critical business data and test the backups periodically.

Why: Backups can help the company recover important data after data loss, system failure, or a security incident.

4. Develop a Disaster Recovery Plan

Create a documented plan for recovering critical systems and data after a major disruption.

Why: A disaster recovery plan can reduce downtime and help employees know what to do during an incident.

5. Improve Password Security

Update the existing password policy and implement a centralized password management system with stronger requirements.

Why: Stronger password controls can reduce the risk of unauthorized account access and make password management more consistent.

6. Implement Separation of Duties

Separate important responsibilities so that sensitive tasks are not controlled by only one person.Role based access should be implemented.

Why: This provides additional oversight and reduces the risk of unauthorized actions.

7. Deploy an Intrusion Detection System

Implement an IDS to monitor for potentially suspicious network activity.

Why: An IDS can provide additional visibility into possible security threats that may not be identified by other controls.

8. Improve Legacy System Management

Create a regular schedule for monitoring and maintaining legacy systems and document intervention procedures.

Why: A consistent maintenance process can help reduce risks associated with outdated systems.

9. Improve Asset Inventory and Classification

Create and maintain an inventory of company hardware, software, systems, and sensitive data. Classify assets based on their importance and sensitivity.

Why: Knowing what assets exist helps the IT department determine what needs protection and which assets are most important to business operations.

10. Review Access and Compliance Regularly

Regularly review user permissions, security controls, and compliance requirements. Remove unnecessary access and update controls when business operations change.

Why: Regular reviews help identify security gaps and prevent outdated permissions or controls from remaining in place.

Conclusion:

Botium Toys already has several controls in place, including a firewall, antivirus software, locks, CCTV, and fire detection systems. However, improvements are needed in access management, encryption, backups, disaster recovery, password management, asset management, and monitoring.

Implementing these improvements would help reduce security risks and strengthen the company's overall security posture.

Note: Botium Toys is a fictional company used for educational purposes. These recommendations are based on the provided training scenario.
