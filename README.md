
# Project Overview 

This project is an educational assessment based on the provided scenario.
The audit was completed using the provided Botium Toys risk assessment, control categories, and controls/compliance checklist as part of the cybersecurity training activity by Google Cybersecurity Course on Coursera.
The purpose of the audit was to review the company's existing security controls and compliance practices and identify areas that need improvement.

# Scenario:
 
This scenario is based on a fictional company:

Botium Toys is a small U.S. business that develops and sells toys. The business has a single physical location, which serves as their main office, a storefront, and warehouse for their products. However, Botium Toy’s online presence has grown, attracting customers in the U.S. and abroad. As a result, their information technology (IT) department is under increasing pressure to support their online market worldwide. 

The manager of the IT department has decided that an internal IT audit needs to be conducted.  
The IT manager starts by implementing the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF), establishing an audit scope and goals, listing assets currently managed by the IT department, and completing a risk assessment. The goal of the audit is to provide an overview of the risks and/or fines that the company might experience due to the current state of their security posture.

# Risk Assessment

The provided risk assessment gives Botium Toys an overall risk score of 8 out of 10.
The main concern is the lack of appropriate controls around sensitive data, access management, backups, and compliance.

# Compliance Areas

The assessment considers selected practices related to:

* Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)

* General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

* System and Organization Controls (SOC)

# Objectives:

The main objectives of this audit were to:

1.Review the assets managed by the IT department.

2.Identify potential security risks and vulnerabilities.

3.Evaluate existing security controls.

4.Review compliance-related requirements.

5.Identify areas where security controls could be improved.

6.Provide recommendations to help reduce organizational risks.


# Key Findings

The assessment identified several areas requiring improvement:

- Least privilege
- Encryption
- Backups
- Disaster recovery
- Password management
- Separation of duties
- Intrusion detection
- Asset classification


# Recommendations:

Based on the assessment, Botium Toys should prioritize the following improvements:

1.Implement least privilege access and separation of duties (Role based access)

2.Encrypt sensitive customer and payment information for confidentiality.

3.Create regular backups of critical data .

4.Develop and test a disaster recovery plan.

5.Implement centralized password management with stronger password requirements.

6.Deploy an intrusion detection system to improve detection of suspicious activity.

7.Create a documented schedule for legacy system maintenance.

8.Classify and inventory company assets and sensitive information.

9.Regularly review user access and security controls.

10.Continue maintaining the physical security controls that are already in place.

# What I learned:

This project helped me understand how a security audit can be used to identify gaps in an organization's existing controls.
One of the main things I noticed was that having a security control in place does not necessarily mean that the control is strong or meets current security requirements.
For example: Botium Toys has a password policy, but the policy is outdated and is not supported by a centralized password management system.

This activity helped me understand that cybersecurity is not only about technical tools. A secure organization also needs appropriate policies, access controls, employee awareness, data protection, compliance practices, and plans for responding to incidents.
